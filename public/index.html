The Senior Engineering Portfolio Standard

How senior and staff engineers at top technology companies actually structure their portfolios to prove competence without fluff.

The Researched Portfolio Blueprint

Hero → About → Experience → 2-4 Finished Case Studies → Open Source → Writing & Talks → Honest Skills → Identifiable Recommendations → Contact + CV
01

Problem & Constraints

Specify the real engineering friction: What was failing, slow, or unmaintainable? State authentic scale constraints rather than generic tutorial descriptions.

02

Decisions & Trade-Offs

Real engineering is defined by what you choose not to do. Document what architecture you chose and which alternatives you rejected and why.

03

Measurable Result + Defensible Number

No hand-waving claims. Provide an auditable, defensible number (e.g. 4.8k req/s @ p99 38ms). If confidential under NDA, state "Confidential" — never invent percentages.

04

Micro-Attribution

Skip solo-hero exaggerations. Honestly declare what you personally engineered versus what teammates, templates, or existing libraries handled.

05

AI-Assistance Disclosure

Radical transparency: specify where AI helped (boilerplate, regex, mock tests) versus what you engineered, and who made the final architectural decisions.

06

Verifiable Links & Receipts

Every case study links directly to Live Demo, Git Repository, and Technical README, alongside timestamped commit SHAs and benchmark logs.

Ethical NDA Handling How to Showcase Confidential Work

If work is covered by client confidentiality: anonymize the client to industry and scale (e.g. "Tier-1 Payment Gateway • ~10M MAU"), keep the technical constraints and your contribution 100% specific, and mark proprietary metrics as "Confidential". Never invent fake percentages or reveal protected client IP.

AI Core Verification

Claim-Evidence Corroboration Engine

Automated semantic vector analysis that compares your stated technical claims against attached commit SHAs, PR diffs, and benchmark logs. Evaluated into three honest tiers:

✓ Supported ≥ 60% Match

Corroborated by Receipts

Stated problem, decisions, and measurable outcomes directly match attached commit hashes, benchmark metrics, or production pull requests.

• Unclear 30% – 59% Match

Partial Evidence Provided

Some claims have matching receipts, but key architectural trade-offs or performance claims lack specific verifying links or execution logs.

× Unsupported < 30% Match

Missing Corroboration

No relevant external artefacts, code diffs, or benchmark data were attached to substantiate the stated claims.
