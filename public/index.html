How It Works in 3 Clear Steps

A streamlined engineering workflow designed for freelancers, developers, and students who build real systems.

STEP 01

Document the Problem & Trade-Offs

Skip marketing jargon. Detail the exact problem constraints and technical bottlenecks. Document the decisions you made and the specific alternatives you rejected. That is the true signature of authentic engineering.

STEP 02

Anchor with Dated Receipts

Attach immutable receipts: GitHub commit SHAs, timestamped pull requests, early Figma drafts, staging preview URLs, or benchmark terminal logs. Genuine proof carries timestamps.

STEP 03

One-Tap Client Confirmation

Generate an un-falsifiable confirmation link. Send it via Slack, WhatsApp, or Email. The client taps "Yes, I Confirm Deliverable" in seconds with zero login required, immediately updating your public verified seal.
