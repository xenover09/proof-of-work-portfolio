The Trust Crisis: AI vs Real Work

Recruiters aur clients generic bullet points dekh kar thak chuke hain. Anyone can prompt an LLM to write "Architected scalable microservices". Here is how Proof-of-Work separates true builders from prompt copy-pasters.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai (3 Simple Steps)

A structured workflow designed for freelancers, students, and engineers who actually build.

STEP 01

Document the Messy Reality

Don't write marketing fluff. State the raw problem, the engineering/design decision you took, and crucially, the alternatives you rejected and why. That's what proves you actually reasoned through it.

STEP 02

Anchor with Dated Artefacts

Attach immutable receipts: GitHub commit SHAs, timestamped pull requests, early Figma drafts, staging preview links, or benchmark terminal logs. Proof has dates.

STEP 03

1-Tap Client Confirmation

Generate a unique client-confirmation link. Send it via WhatsApp, Slack, or Email. The client taps "Yes, Confirmed" in 5 seconds without signing up. The verified badge immediately updates on your public portfolio.
