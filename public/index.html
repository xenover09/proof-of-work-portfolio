How It Works

Three Steps to Unshakeable Credibility

Built for software developers, freelancers, and technical students who want their work to speak for itself.

01

Document Problem & Trade-Offs

Skip marketing jargon. State the authentic bottleneck, the choice you made, and crucially, which alternatives you rejected and why. That is the authentic fingerprint of real engineering.

02

Attach Dated Receipts

Provide immutable evidence: Git commit SHAs, timestamped pull request links, staging previews, or terminal benchmark outputs. Genuine proof has verifiable timestamps.

03

1-Tap Client Authentication

Issue a private confirmation link. Send it via WhatsApp, Slack, or Email. Your client taps "Yes, I Confirm Deliverable" in 5 seconds with zero signup required. Your portfolio turns green instantly.
